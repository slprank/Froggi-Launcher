Froggi

The stream and tournament tool for Super Smash Bros. Melee — live Slippi overlays for OBS, stage striking on players' phones, a tournament dashboard and automation. Free and open source.

For streamers

Overlays that follow the game by themselves — no clicking during a match.

Live overlaysPercent, stocks, characters, score, rank, win chance, fight stats and more — build your own in the editor or start from a demo (HUD, scoreboard, doubles, post-game stats).
OBS in one clickFroggi connects to OBS, adds your overlay as a browser source and switches scenes for menus, games, post-game and sets.
Overlays on the gameDraw your HUD straight on the Dolphin window — no OBS needed (Windows).
HighlightsFroggi spots punishes and combos live and plays the best moments from OBS's replay buffer after each game, set or event.
Ranked & sessionsSlippi rank, rating changes and session wins/losses — shown on stream as they happen.
Singles & doublesEvery player element works for all four doubles players, with team names, team colours and team stocks.

For tournaments

Run sets from one dashboard while the stream keeps up.

Stage striking on phonesEach player scans a link: character pick, rock-paper-scissors, strikes, bans and counterpicks with DSR — shown live on stream.
Tournament dashboardSet score, best of, player and team names, fixing a set by hand, and loading earlier replays into a set.
Automatic scoresWins are reported from the Slippi replays, in singles and doubles.

Automate & play

Make Froggi react to the game exactly the way you want.

FlowsWhen → And → Then cards: switch scenes, call webhooks, control OBS, run timers and build highlight reels from game events.
WebhooksSend game events to Discord bots, dashboards or your own tools.
AI assistantConnect Claude (or another AI app) to build overlays, set up OBS and fix problems by chatting.
MinigamesBingo and Iron Man — solo, local or online against a friend, with a live overlay.
Remote controlControl scenes and overlays from your phone, at home or over the internet.
No accountRuns on your computer. Free forever, open source.
